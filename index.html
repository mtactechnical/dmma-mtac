Ratings Forming Part of a Watch in a Manned Engine-room (RFPWME) equips engine-room ratings with the knowledge and practical skills necessary to perform assigned watchkeeping duties safely and efficiently. Ratings Forming Part of a Navigational Watch (RFPNW)

This course provides an in-depth examination of the principles and practices governing the role of a Rating Forming Part of a Navigational Watch (RFPNW) , strictly in accordance with STCW Regulation II/4 . Participants will acquire the requisite competencies in helmmanship, look-out procedures, and bridge support functions to ensure the safe and efficient navigation of the vessel.

Security Awareness Training & Seafarers with Designated Security Duties (SAT-SDSD)

This combined program delivers comprehensive training in both STCW Security Awareness and Designated Security Duties, preparing seafarers to fulfill their roles under the International Ship and Port Security (ISPS) Code . Participants will first master the foundational skills to maintain heightened vigilance, recognize potential security threats, and understand reporting procedures , before advancing to the specific competencies required for crew members assigned security responsibilities, including conducting ship searches, implementing security procedures, and contributing to the effective implementation of the Ship Security Plan.

Updating Course on BT-PSSR (UC BT-PSSR)

This updating course provides existing seafarers with the mandatory training to align their certification with the expanded STCW competencies for Personal Safety and Social Responsibilities (PSSR) effective 1 January 2026 . Participants will acquire the requisite knowledge and understanding to contribute to the prevention of and response to violence and harassment, including bullying and sexual assault, thereby reinforcing a culture of safety, respect, and inclusivity within the maritime profession.

In-House Maritime Training Courses

Consolidated Marine Pollution (MARPOL)

This comprehensive course provides deck and engineer officers with an in-depth analysis of the consolidated International Convention for the Prevention of Pollution from Ships (MARPOL), covering Annexes I through VI to ensure full compliance with pollution prevention requirements . Participants will acquire the advanced competencies to monitor and control shipboard operations in accordance with legislative mandates, including proper management of bilge water, ballast water, and garbage, as well as accurate record-keeping in the Oil and Garbage Record Books, thereby fulfilling the standards of competence prescribed in STCW Tables A-II/1, II/2, III/1, and III/2.

Enroll Now

Advanced Training for Stability and Trim (STAB & TRIM)

This advanced course provides senior deck officers with the comprehensive knowledge and proficiency to control trim, stability, and stress at the management level, in accordance with STCW Code Section A-II/2 and Table A-II/2 . Participants will master the theories and factors affecting trim and stability, learn to perform advanced calculations for intact and damage stability using both traditional methods and stability software, and understand the critical measures necessary to preserve stability in all conditions, including emergency situations.

Professional Development Courses

IMO Model Course 6.09

This IMO Model Course 6.09 provides maritime professionals with the advanced pedagogical competencies to deliver STCW-compliant training, equipping them with the theoretical foundations and practical methodologies for effective course design, instructional delivery, and trainee assessment . Participants will master the systematic processes of identifying training needs, developing comprehensive lesson plans, and evaluating learning outcomes, thereby meeting the qualification requirements for instructors in Maritime Higher Education Institutions and Maritime Training Institutes.

Enroll Now

IMO Model Course 3.12

This IMO Model Course 3.12 provides maritime administrators, trainers, and assessors with the essential competencies to implement STCW training, assessment, and certification requirements, covering international provisions, selection of assessment methodologies, organization of examinations, and the issuance and control of certificates . Participants will master the systematic processes for evaluating seafarer competence, developing written and practical tests, and maintaining uniform certification standards to ensure the effective implementation of the STCW Convention under national law.

IMO Model Course 6.10

This IMO Model Course 6.10 provides maritime instructors and assessors with the specialized pedagogical competencies to administer, supervise, and evaluate simulator-based training and assessment in accordance with STCW Regulations I/6 and I/12 . Participants will master the systematic processes of planning, creating, executing, and evaluating simulation exercises, integrating principles of simulator pedagogy and effective communication to ensure the achievement of competencies set forth in the STCW Convention.

About DMMA Maritime Training and Assessment Center

The Maritime Training and Assessment Center at DMMA College of Southern Philippines is a premier institution dedicated to shaping the next generation of maritime professionals. With state-of-the-art facilities and experienced faculty, the center offers comprehensive training programs that encompass both theoretical knowledge and practical skills essential for a successful career at sea. Students undergo rigorous training in navigation, seamanship, marine engineering, and safety protocols, preparing them to meet the challenges of the maritime industry with confidence and competence.

Beyond its foundational courses for cadets and new entrants, the center plays a vital role in the continuous professional development of current industry professionals. It serves as a key hub for experienced seafarers who are required to complete mandatory refresher courses to maintain their qualifications and ensure compliance with international maritime regulations. These advanced programs allow seasoned officers and crew to update their competencies in critical areas such as personal survival techniques, advanced firefighting, survival craft and rescue boats. By providing these essential upgrading opportunities, the DMMA Maritime Training and Assessment Center ensures that the Filipino seafarer remains globally competitive and at the forefront of maritime safety and excellence.
